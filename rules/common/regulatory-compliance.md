# Regulatory Compliance

All trading strategies, execution processes, and reporting must comply with applicable financial regulations. When in doubt, err on the side of caution and flag for compliance review.

## MiFID II Best Execution

- Document the execution venue selection rationale for every trade.
- Consider price, costs, speed, likelihood of execution, settlement, size, and nature of the order.
- Maintain records of execution quality metrics for all venues used.
- Provide best execution reports to clients upon request.
- Review and update the execution policy at least annually.

## Dodd-Frank Reporting

- Report all swap transactions to a registered swap data repository within required timeframes.
- Maintain records of all OTC derivative positions and mark-to-market valuations.
- Comply with clearing requirements for standardized swaps.
- Monitor and report large trader positions as required by CFTC.

## Position Limits

- Respect all exchange-imposed position limits for futures and options contracts.
- Monitor aggregate positions across accounts and entities to avoid exceeding limits.
- Apply for hedge exemptions where applicable and document the hedging rationale.
- For commodities, comply with CFTC speculative position limits.

## Market Abuse Prevention

- Never trade on material non-public information (MNPI). When in doubt, do not trade.
- Maintain information barriers between research, trading, and advisory functions.
- Monitor for patterns that could be construed as market manipulation.
- Report suspicious trading activity to compliance immediately.
- Document the rationale for all trades, especially those around corporate events.

## Short-Selling Rules

- Comply with locate requirements before executing short sales.
- Respect short-selling bans and restrictions in applicable jurisdictions.
- Mark all sell orders correctly as long, short, or short exempt.
- Monitor fail-to-deliver positions and take corrective action.

## Insider Trading Rules

- Maintain restricted lists for securities about which the firm has MNPI.
- Pre-clear all personal trading for covered employees.
- Enforce blackout periods around material corporate events.
- Train all personnel on insider trading policies at least annually.

## Audit Trail Requirements

- Maintain complete audit trails of all order generation, routing, and execution.
- Record all communications related to trading decisions.
- Retain records for the minimum required period (typically 5-7 years).
- Ensure records are stored in a tamper-proof format and are readily accessible for regulatory examination.
- Log all algorithmic trading decisions, including the parameters and data inputs that generated each signal.
